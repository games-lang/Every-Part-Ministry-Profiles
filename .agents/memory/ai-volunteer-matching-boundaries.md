---
name: AI ministry assistance boundaries
description: Privacy and trust rules for using Ministry Profiles in AI-assisted matching and team brainstorming.
---

AI may prioritize only same-church candidate IDs that already have locally verified evidence for the ministry need. For team brainstorming, send only aggregated, server-owned canonical signal labels shared by multiple profiles, and accept only known signal keys back. Never send names, contact details, demographics, spiritual-health answers, life experiences, custom labels, optional focus text, or free-text profile responses. Generate every displayed name, purpose, and reason locally from verified signals.

**Why:** Ministry Profiles contain pastoral and potentially identifying free text. Public or custom text can carry PII or prompt injection, and model-generated prose can be fabricated or manipulated. Canonical aggregates plus locally generated claims keep recommendations explainable and advisory.

**How to apply:** Use these boundaries for volunteer search, team brainstorming, ranking changes, new providers, or expanded fields. Treat AI output as bounded key selection or prioritization, never as placement, calling, willingness, or certainty.
