---
name: PartFinder leadership personalization
description: Trust boundary for pastor-specific coaching context and memory.
---

PartFinder leadership personalization must be per authenticated pastor, visible, editable, and pausable. It may use only context the pastor intentionally saved; conversation text must never become hidden memory.

**Why:** Ministry leaders need personalized guidance without invisible psychological profiling, and different leaders in the same church may have different priorities and communication preferences.

**How to apply:** Keep church data separate from leadership preferences, omit saved context entirely when personalization is paused, treat saved text as untrusted data rather than instructions, and never infer sensitive traits or spiritual authority.

Leadership text may inform guidance as explicitly disclosed data, but must stay local for find-people and team suggestions. Matching preferences only lift already evidence-qualified candidates modestly; explicit team-request focus takes precedence over saved priorities. Pausing also resets communication style and length to defaults. Unassigned lists and church insights remain unpersonalized.

**Why:** The user required visible, honest personalization without sending saved leadership free text into matching/team AI requests or letting preferences replace participant evidence.

**How to apply:** Preserve disabled-profile behavior exactly, make claims only when returned results actually received the local effect, and use canonical signal keys rather than leadership text in matching/team AI payloads.
