---
name: Strengths reflection framing
description: Product language and interpretation rules for the natural-strengths assessment.
---

Present natural strengths through original, plain-language reflection statements inspired only by the general idea of strengths-based development. Do not claim affiliation with, copy, or closely imitate a branded strengths assessment. Results are conversation starters, not diagnoses or placement recommendations.

**Why:** The user wants a strengths-based experience loosely connected to familiar strengths frameworks without making it identical to a commercial assessment.

**How to apply:** Keep prompts original, store response patterns for discussion, show only approachable strength labels in results, and retain the non-diagnostic disclaimer in member, pastor, print, and reporting views.
