---
name: Completed profile portrait
description: Narrative hierarchy and synthesis rule for completed adult participant and leader results.
---

Completed adult results must build one cumulative ministry portrait in this order: part in the Body, ministry tendency, gifts, heart, story, current season, where the signals come together, and the next conversation.

**Why:** The product should help a person and pastor understand one coherent story about contribution to the Body, not present several disconnected assessment reports or optimize for filling positions.

**How to apply:** Treat APEST, gifts, personality, strengths, passions, experience, and availability as evidence feeding the portrait. Avoid separate raw-assessment sections, repeated explanations, and opaque matches. Participant and leader views should share the same story arc while using appropriate pronouns and preserving leader-only content.

The whole-profile synthesis must mention only configuration-enabled saved signals. Ministry opportunities begin with interests the participant named, remain advisory, and are tempered by the current season. Spiritual Health belongs within current-season pastoral context rather than as a competing assessment chapter.

**Why:** The result should support discovery, development, and engagement while avoiding fixed identities, readiness judgments, or placement claims.

**How to apply:** Hide disabled chapters instead of showing empty placeholders. Keep the dedicated privacy-safe infographic as the adult print output even when the leader tab is active.
