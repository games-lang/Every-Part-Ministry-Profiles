---
name: Early Access boundaries
description: Durable privacy and onboarding rules for the Every Part Early Access program.
---

Early Access onboarding acknowledgement belongs to the individual signed-in church user, not to the church as a single shared timestamp. Owner-facing Early Access insights should remain aggregate and activity-oriented, with no member names, profile content, or church records exposed outside tenant access.

**Why:** Churches may have multiple administrators, and Early Access is intended to improve the product without weakening the privacy boundaries that protect ministry and member information.

**How to apply:** Add new Early Access features behind server-enforced church/user authorization. Prefer counts, rates, activity windows, and health labels over raw records in owner views. New churches entering the program are Early Access and founding churches by default; future Standard transitions must be explicit.
