---
name: Ministry tendency scoring
description: Interpretation and scoring boundaries for the original six-part How You Tend to Minister framework.
---

Use six categories: Hands / Doer, Ears / Listener, Shoulders / Supporter, Voice / Communicator, Arms / Connector, and Backbone / Organizer. Score them primarily from enabled “How You Tend to Operate” dimensions. Enabled natural strengths may add a small capped supporting signal; APEST orientations and spiritual gifts must not alter these scores.

Retain raw scores, normalized scores, the primary result, a close secondary result, score spread, and a plain-language confidence level in the derived result. Do not display percentages as if the framework were a validated psychometric instrument. Say a person “tends to minister like” a body part, never that they are that body part.

**Why:** The framework is meant to describe how someone naturally engages people and responsibility, independently from their contribution orientation and gifts. Overweighting other assessments would collapse distinct signals into one result and overstate precision.

**How to apply:** Keep the scoring deterministic and configuration-aware. Missing or disabled dimensions must not be inferred. Show a secondary result only when scores are genuinely close, and always include the non-fixed-identity disclaimer.
