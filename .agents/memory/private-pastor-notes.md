---
name: Private pastor notes
description: Privacy and synthesis boundaries for leader-authored notes and Ministry Leader View guidance.
---

Pastor notes are a separate, author-scoped leader resource tied to one adult profile and church. A trusted leader may read and update only their own note. Notes must never be embedded in the Ministry Profile itself or exposed through general profile, participant, journey, youth, print, AI-helper, or PartFinder responses.

**Why:** Notes can contain private pastoral context that the participant did not submit as assessment data and that another leader should not inherit implicitly.

**How to apply:** Keep note access behind an explicit trusted-leader allowlist and enforce profile/church/author scope in the same database operation. Treat participant and unknown roles as denied.

Leader fit suggestions may begin only from a configuration-enabled ministry interest the participant named. Expose ability, interest, calling signals, availability, and experience separately, including absent evidence; limited capacity must temper the suggestion.

**Why:** Leaders need transparent reasons for a conversation, not opaque matches that can be mistaken for assignment, calling, readiness, or spiritual maturity.

**How to apply:** Derive suggestions at display time from saved profile snapshots, use advisory language, and never invent a ministry area from an APEST result, gift, strength, or tendency alone.
