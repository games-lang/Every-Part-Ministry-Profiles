---
name: Logo color authority
description: Brand rule for coordinating the Every Part logo and application palette
---

Keep the original Every Part logo artwork and colors unchanged. When the website and logo feel inconsistent, adjust the shared website palette to complement the logo rather than recoloring or replacing the logo.

**Why:** The original logo is the fixed brand source of truth; changing it creates unwanted brand drift.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing logo asset and shared Brand component behavior. Use the logo’s navy, teal, gold, blue, and green as the reference when changing home-page or signed-in application color tokens.
