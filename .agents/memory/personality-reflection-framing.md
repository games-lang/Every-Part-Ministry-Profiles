---
name: Personality reflection framing
description: Interpretation and presentation rules for the ministry-focused personality assessment.
---

Present personality through seven original spectra: social energy, decision lens, planning style, focus style, action style, pace preference, and work style. Show percentages and degree-of-tendency language rather than rigid types.

**Why:** Personality should help members understand how they naturally operate without copying a branded framework or implying that a result determines calling, spiritual gifts, value, or permanent limitations.

**How to apply:** Keep questions and explanations original, positive, balanced, and ministry-focused. Include a combined summary, practical ministry implications, and an explicit reminder that God can work through both natural patterns and areas of growth.
