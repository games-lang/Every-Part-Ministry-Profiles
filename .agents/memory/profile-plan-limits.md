---
name: Profile plan limits
description: How church subscription limits count profiles and behave after plan changes.
---

During early access, every church can create unlimited completed adult, Discover, Explore, and Develop Ministry Profiles, regardless of its stored billing plan. Existing and newly created churches follow the same rule. Planned future capacities remain Starter 5, Growing 50, Complete 100, Network 250, and Unlimited with no cap; they are not currently enforced. AI credit limits are separate and still apply.

**Why:** The user requested unlimited profiles for all churches for now, without changing a church’s billing plan or AI allowance. Planned pricing should remain clear without implying those future profile limits are active today.

**How to apply:** Do not block adult or youth profile submissions for capacity during early access, and show current profile usage as unlimited. Keep planned limits visibly marked as future. If enforcement is deliberately restored later, count all completed pathways and enforce new submissions transactionally; never delete or hide existing profiles after a downgrade.
