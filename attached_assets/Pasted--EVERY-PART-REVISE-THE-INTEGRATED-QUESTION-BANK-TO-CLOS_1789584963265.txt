# EVERY PART — REVISE THE INTEGRATED QUESTION BANK TO CLOSE COVERAGE GAPS

Thank you. Do NOT implement the assessment yet.

The current design direction is approved:

- 43 integrated behavior questions
- 21 Personality questions
- 15 optional Spiritual Experience & Discernment prompts
- Four distinct scoring categories
- Mixed participant flow
- Church Spiritual Gift toggles
- Historical profile protection
- Adult-only pilot

However, the content report correctly shows that the current bank does not yet provide enough evidence for every construct.

Do NOT weaken the evidence thresholds merely to keep the assessment shorter.

The next task is to revise the question bank strategically so every scored construct has enough defensible evidence.

==================================================
KEEP THE 21 PERSONALITY QUESTIONS
==================================================

Keep the current 21 neutral Personality questions:

7 spectra × 3 questions each.

Do NOT reduce these to 15 simply to shorten the assessment.

Personality should remain independently measured and should not be inferred from ministry behaviors.

Only rewrite a Personality question if there is a clarity, translation, balance, or scoring problem.

==================================================
FOCUS THIS REVISION ON APEST, GIFTS, AND STRENGTHS
==================================================

Do not rewrite the entire assessment.

Start with the existing integrated behavior bank.

Identify only the constructs that currently lack sufficient evidence.

For each gap, determine whether it can responsibly be solved by:

1. improving an existing question,
2. adding a legitimate Secondary mapping to an existing question,
3. replacing a weak/redundant question,
4. or adding a new focused question.

Prefer improving or replacing existing questions before adding large numbers of new ones.

However, do NOT create artificial cross-mappings simply to hit coverage numbers.

==================================================
APEST REQUIREMENT
==================================================

Every APEST orientation must have enough meaningful evidence.

Target:

6–8 strong indicators per orientation.

The five orientations are:

- Apostle
- Prophet
- Evangelist
- Shepherd
- Teacher

Prefer a mix of:

- direct Primary indicators
- a smaller number of genuinely supportive Secondary indicators

Avoid giving one orientation dramatically more evidence than another.

Do not count an item merely because it has a superficial relationship to the orientation.

The question should actually help distinguish that orientation.

==================================================
SPIRITUAL GIFT REQUIREMENT
==================================================

For each ordinary enabled Spiritual Gift that Every Part intends to publish as an assessment result, require:

at least 3 meaningful indicators.

Prefer:

3–5 indicators

where feasible.

A gift should NOT receive a publishable score based on one or two broad overlapping questions.

Do not solve gaps by tagging unrelated questions.

If a Spiritual Gift cannot responsibly be measured within a reasonable questionnaire length, flag it for a different treatment rather than weakening scoring.

Possible treatment options include:

- assessment-scored
- Spiritual Experience module
- conversation/discernment only

Do NOT make that classification change without showing it for review first.

==================================================
STRENGTH REQUIREMENT
==================================================

Every scored Strength should generally have:

at least 3 meaningful indicators.

Prefer:

3–5

when feasible.

Questions should reflect observable ability or recurring capability.

Do not confuse Strengths with:

- Personality
- Spiritual Gifts
- APEST
- spiritual maturity.

==================================================
SPECIAL SPIRITUAL GIFTS
==================================================

Review gifts such as:

- Healing
- Miracles
- Tongues
- Interpretation of Tongues

separately.

The current report correctly notes that contextual questions about humility, guidance, discernment, or openness are NOT themselves evidence that a supernatural gift is present.

Do not count contextual questions as gift indicators.

For a special gift to receive an assessment result, require actual experience-based evidence appropriate to that gift.

Otherwise display it only as:

"An area for conversation or discernment"

rather than publishing a scored gift result.

Preserve:

"Not sure / I have not experienced this / this is not part of my tradition"

as an unscored response.

==================================================
REVIEW QUESTIONS WITH TOO MANY MAPPINGS
==================================================

The report identified four questions with five proposed mappings.

Review each one individually.

As a general guideline, an integrated behavior question should normally have no more than:

- 1 Primary APEST mapping
- optionally 1 Secondary APEST mapping
- 1 Primary Spiritual Gift mapping
- optionally 1 Secondary Gift mapping
- 1 Primary Strength mapping
- optionally 1 Secondary Strength mapping

But do not automatically use all of these.

Most questions should have fewer mappings.

Five mappings are acceptable only when each relationship is strong and independently defensible.

For each of the four high-mapping questions, provide:

- question text
- all mappings
- why each mapping exists
- which mappings could be removed
- your recommended final mapping

==================================================
DO NOT USE MAPPING COUNT AS THE GOAL
==================================================

A question that scores three constructs well is better than a question that scores six constructs weakly.

Optimize for:

CONCEPTUAL QUALITY

not:

MAXIMUM REUSE.

The purpose of integrated scoring is to reduce unnecessary duplicate questions, not to make every response score everything.

==================================================
TARGET LENGTH
==================================================

The current 64 core questions are not a hard ceiling.

If coverage cannot responsibly be achieved at 64, add focused questions.

Prefer a final core assessment somewhere around:

65–75 questions

if that provides substantially better evidence.

It is acceptable to exceed this range slightly if the production taxonomy genuinely requires it.

Do not add questions just to reach a target.

Also do not keep the bank artificially short if doing so weakens the results.

The guiding principle remains:

Use the shortest assessment that still produces sufficiently supported ministry-discernment patterns.

==================================================
LOOK FOR REDUNDANCY
==================================================

Before adding a question, inspect the current bank for questions that are:

- highly repetitive
- weakly worded
- measuring almost the same behavior
- carrying little unique scoring value.

Where possible:

replace one weak/redundant item with a stronger question that closes a coverage gap.

This is preferable to endlessly increasing total length.

==================================================
PRIMARY VS SECONDARY EVIDENCE
==================================================

Continue using:

Primary mapping = 1.0

Secondary mapping = 0.5

However, coverage reports must distinguish between them.

Do not claim a construct has six equally strong indicators if:

2 are Primary

and

4 are weak Secondary mappings.

For every construct show:

- number of Primary indicators
- number of Secondary indicators
- total weighted evidence opportunities.

==================================================
QUESTION QUALITY TEST
==================================================

For every newly written or substantially revised behavior question, test:

1. Is this one clear behavior?

2. Could a participant answer from ordinary life, not only church ministry?

3. Does one response sound obviously more spiritual than another?

4. Can it translate cleanly into other languages?

5. Does it actually distinguish the intended construct?

6. Is it substantially different from other questions?

7. Are the proposed mappings directly supported by the behavior?

8. Could someone score high for a legitimate reason unrelated to the intended construct?

If the final question creates significant ambiguity, rewrite it.

==================================================
CULTURAL AND MULTILINGUAL REVIEW
==================================================

Every Part is intended for multilingual and multicultural churches.

Review the question bank for behaviors that may be strongly influenced by:

- American church culture
- leadership culture
- educational background
- communication style
- economic circumstances
- cultural expectations around authority
- introversion/extroversion
- access to ministry opportunities.

Avoid rewarding one cultural style as spiritually superior.

Use plain, concrete wording suitable for translation.

==================================================
RESULT PUBLISHING RULE
==================================================

Keep the minimum-evidence rule.

Do NOT publish a scored construct when there is insufficient evidence.

Instead display:

"Not enough information yet."

This is preferable to giving someone an unreliable result.

==================================================
CONVERSATION-ONLY CONSTRUCTS
==================================================

If after careful design a construct cannot be responsibly measured with at least three meaningful indicators without making the assessment excessively long or repetitive:

do NOT invent weak questions.

Flag the construct.

Recommend whether it should become:

A. Assessment-scored

B. Optional experience/discernment module

C. Conversation-only / community-discerned

Provide the reasoning.

Do not change its classification until approved.
