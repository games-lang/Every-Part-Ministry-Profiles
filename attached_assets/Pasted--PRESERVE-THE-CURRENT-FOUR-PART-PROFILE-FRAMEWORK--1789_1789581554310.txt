==================================================
PRESERVE THE CURRENT FOUR-PART PROFILE FRAMEWORK
==================================================

CRITICAL:

Although the participant will now complete ONE integrated assessment, the finished Ministry Profile must continue to organize the assessment results into the same four conceptual areas used by Every Part.

Preserve these four sections and their meaning:

1. HOW YOU TEND TO MINISTER
   APEST

   This describes the person's general ministry orientation:
   Apostle
   Prophet
   Evangelist
   Shepherd
   Teacher

2. HOW GOD HAS EQUIPPED YOU
   Spiritual Gifts

   This describes spiritual gifts that may be present and worth exploring through prayer, service, fruit, and confirmation from Christian community.

3. WHAT YOU ARE NATURALLY GOOD AT
   Strengths

   This describes observable capabilities and things the person tends to do naturally or effectively.

4. HOW YOU TEND TO OPERATE
   Personality

   This describes preferences and tendencies in how the person relates, communicates, makes decisions, approaches structure, responds to change, and works with others.

DO NOT rename these four categories without approval.

DO NOT combine them into one category such as:

"Your Wiring"
"Your Ministry Type"
"Your Ministry Personality"
or
"Your Overall Score."

These four areas answer four different questions:

How do I tend to minister?
→ APEST

How has God equipped me?
→ Spiritual Gifts

What am I naturally good at?
→ Strengths

How do I tend to operate?
→ Personality

The integrated assessment changes HOW the information is gathered.

It does NOT change HOW Every Part understands or presents the four parts of the person's profile.

The participant may answer questions in a blended order so the assessment feels natural and avoids repetition.

However, after completion, the results must be sorted back into these four clearly distinct sections.

Part Finder and ministry leaders must also retain these distinctions when viewing or using profile data.
