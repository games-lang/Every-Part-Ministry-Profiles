Please improve the main public homepage for Every Part so it is more compelling to pastors and church leaders and more likely to make them want to try the platform.

The goal is not just to explain features. The homepage should clearly communicate the problem Every Part solves, the biblical vision behind it, how the process works, and what church leaders gain from using it.

Please preserve the existing app functionality, login flow, dashboard, member profile logic, database, and user data. Focus on the public-facing website and homepage experience.

CORE POSITIONING

Every Part is not simply a spiritual gifts test.

Every Part is a prayerful ministry mobilization platform that helps churches understand how God has shaped their people and connect them with meaningful opportunities to serve and grow.

Use this core message prominently:

“Every person has a part. Help them discover it.”

Support it with language such as:

“Every Part helps churches prayerfully discover the gifts, passions, calling, ministry potential, and availability of their people—and connect them with a place to serve and grow.”

HERO SECTION

Keep the opening very simple and clear.

Headline:

“Every person has a part. Help them discover it.”

Supporting copy:

“Every Part helps churches prayerfully discover how God has shaped their people and connect them with meaningful places to serve and grow.”

Primary button:
“Try Every Part”

Secondary button:
“See How It Works”

Please include a visual preview of the product in the hero if possible, such as a sample Ministry Profile or leader dashboard preview using fictional/demo data only.

A pastor should understand the purpose of Every Part within 5–10 seconds.

ADD A CLEAR PROBLEM SECTION

Create a section with this idea:

“Your church is full of people God has already equipped. The challenge is helping them discover where they fit.”

Show several common church challenges:

* Hidden gifts in the congregation
* The same small group of people serving everywhere
* Members who want to serve but do not know where to begin
* Pastors recruiting mostly from people they already know
* Potential leaders going unnoticed
* People serving in roles that may not fit their gifts or current season

Close this section with:

“Every Part helps move people from discovery to meaningful ministry.”

ADD THE EVERY PART PATHWAY

Create a visually strong section titled:

“The Every Part Pathway”

Use these six steps:

1. PRAY
   “Begin by asking God for wisdom, openness, and direction.”

2. SET UP
   “Customize Every Part around your church, ministry teams, and serving opportunities.”

3. DISCOVER
   “Members complete their Ministry Profile and reflect on their gifts, passions, ministry style, experience, availability, spiritual health, and more.”

4. DISCERN
   “Leaders meet with members to hear their story, discuss their profile, pray together, and discern possible next steps.”

5. CONNECT
   “Help each person find a ministry team that fits their calling, gifts, passions, and current season of life.”

6. DEVELOP
   “Follow up, encourage growth, develop emerging leaders, and revisit ministry fit over time.”

Prominently include this statement:

“The profile starts the conversation. Prayer, relationship, and discernment help determine the next step.”

Make the pathway visually easy to scan on desktop and mobile.

“Now you are the body of Christ, and each one of you is a part of it.”
— 1 Corinthians 12:27

Also consider:

“God has placed the parts in the body, every one of them, just as he wanted them to be.”
— 1 Corinthians 12:18

“We have different gifts, according to the grace given to each of us.”
— Romans 12:6

Near the serving or connection section:

Do not overload the homepage with Scripture. Use 3–4 thoughtful Scripture callouts that clearly connect to the purpose of the section.

IMPORTANT

Before making changes:

* Review the current homepage and reuse good existing components.
* Preserve all existing functionality.
* Do not expose real user data.
* Do not change authentication unless necessary.
* Do not make database changes unless required.
* Do not remove existing working features.
* Test all links and buttons afterward.
* Test both desktop and mobile layouts.
* Make sure the finished homepage is polished enough that a pastor visiting for the first time would think:

“I understand what this does.”
“This could help our church.”
“I trust the philosophy behind it.”
“I want to see the profile.”
“I want to try it.”
