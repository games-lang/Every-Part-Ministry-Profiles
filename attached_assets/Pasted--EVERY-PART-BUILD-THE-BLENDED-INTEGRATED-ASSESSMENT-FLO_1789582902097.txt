# EVERY PART — BUILD THE BLENDED INTEGRATED ASSESSMENT FLOW

The finished profile restructure is correct. Keep the current result structure exactly as it now exists, including:

1. How You Tend to Minister — APEST
2. How God Has Equipped You — Spiritual Gifts
3. What You Are Naturally Good At — Strengths
4. How You Tend to Operate — Personality
5. What Moves Your Heart
6. What Your Story Has Prepared You For
7. Your Current Season
8. Where These Things Come Together — including Your Serving Pattern
9. The Conversation

Now redesign the assessment-taking experience for the adult pathway so that the first four assessment areas are completed as ONE blended assessment.

Do not merge the result categories.

The participant should experience one unified questionnaire, while the scoring engine must continue calculating APEST, Spiritual Gifts, Strengths, and Personality independently.

==================================================
BLEND ONLY THESE FOUR AREAS
==================================================

Blend together:

- How You Tend to Minister — APEST
- How God Has Equipped You — Spiritual Gifts
- What You Are Naturally Good At — Strengths
- How You Tend to Operate — Personality

Do NOT weave the following into the blended assessment:

- What Moves Your Heart
- Story
- Season
- Spiritual Health
- Interests
- Availability
- Free-text reflection
- Other existing profile sections

Those should remain separate steps in the profile flow.

==================================================
USE MIXED ROUNDS — NOT FULL RANDOMIZATION
==================================================

Do NOT fully shuffle all questions into a random order.

Instead, organize the blended assessment into several natural mixed rounds.

Each round should contain questions from multiple scoring areas where appropriate.

Suggested participant-facing round names:

1. How You Notice and Respond
2. How You Work With Others
3. How You Communicate and Guide
4. How You Care and Serve
5. How You Think and Approach Change
6. How You Tend to Work

These are user-experience headings only.

They are NOT new scoring categories.

The purpose is to make the assessment feel cohesive without revealing the scoring structure.

==================================================
HIDE THE SCORING CATEGORIES WHILE ANSWERING
==================================================

Participants should NOT see labels such as:

- APEST
- Spiritual Gifts
- Strengths
- Personality

while answering questions.

They should not know which category or construct a question feeds.

Keep all scoring tags hidden.

The participant should feel like they are simply answering one thoughtful set of questions about how they tend to serve, respond, think, relate, and work.

==================================================
KEEP THE FOUR RESULT SYSTEMS SEPARATE
==================================================

Even though the questions are blended visually, continue calculating these independently:

APEST

Spiritual Gifts

Strengths

Personality

Do not create a combined master score.

Do not merge the scoring tables.

Do not flatten them into one result category.

The blended flow is a participant-experience change, not a scoring-category merger.

==================================================
KEEP CHURCH TOGGLES WORKING
==================================================

All current section, subsection, and Spiritual Gift toggles must continue working.

If a church disables an area or construct, adapt the blended flow dynamically.

If a question only measures a disabled construct:

remove that question from the active assessment.

If a question measures both a disabled construct and an enabled construct:

KEEP the question

but disable only the inactive scoring connection.

Example:

A question contributes to:

- Evangelism Spiritual Gift
- Evangelist APEST
- Communication Strength

If the church disables the Evangelism Spiritual Gift:

keep the question if it still contributes to Evangelist APEST and Communication Strength.

Do not calculate or display Evangelism as a Spiritual Gift.

==================================================
SPIRITUAL GIFT ON/OFF SETTINGS MUST REMAIN
==================================================

Continue allowing authorized church leaders to turn individual Spiritual Gifts on or off.

Turning a gift off must:

- hide that gift from current results
- stop scoring that gift
- stop Part Finder from using that gift
- remove gift-only questions when appropriate

Turning a gift off must NOT:

- delete historical answers
- delete historical scores
- rewrite old profiles
- alter previous saved snapshots

Historical assessments must remain tied to the gift configuration that existed when they were completed.

==================================================
SPECIAL SPIRITUAL EXPERIENCE QUESTIONS
==================================================

Do NOT randomly scatter questions about things such as:

- Tongues
- Interpretation of Tongues
- Healing
- Miracles

throughout the normal blended rounds.

If enabled Spiritual Gifts require these questions, place them near the end of the integrated assessment in a short subsection called something like:

SPIRITUAL EXPERIENCE & DISCERNMENT

This remains part of the overall integrated assessment, but it should feel intentionally distinct.

Include an appropriate option such as:

"Not sure / I have not experienced this / this is not part of my church tradition."

That response must remain unscored.

Do not use these spiritual-experience answers to infer Personality unless there is an independently justified scoring reason.

Do not automatically infer APEST from extraordinary spiritual experiences.

==================================================
PERSONALITY QUESTIONS
==================================================

Personality questions can be blended into the ordinary mixed rounds where they fit naturally.

However:

Personality must continue to be scored independently.

Do not infer Personality from spiritual behaviors.

Examples:

Prayer does not automatically mean Conscientiousness.

Mercy does not automatically mean Agreeableness.

Leadership does not automatically mean Extraversion.

Speaking in tongues does not automatically mean Openness.

Public speaking does not automatically mean Extroversion.

==================================================
KEEP HEART, STORY, SEASON, AND SPIRITUAL HEALTH SEPARATE
==================================================

After the integrated assessment, continue the rest of the Ministry Profile as separate reflective steps.

Suggested overall adult flow:

1. Prayer / Profile Introduction

2. Integrated Assessment
   - APEST
   - Spiritual Gifts
   - Strengths
   - Personality

3. What Moves Your Heart

4. What Your Story Has Prepared You For

5. Your Current Season

6. Spiritual Health, if currently enabled

7. Existing interests, availability, experience, language, family, and other profile sections

8. Results / Portrait

9. Synthesis — including Your Serving Pattern

10. Conversation

If the current order of non-assessment profile sections differs, preserve the existing order unless there is a strong UX or technical reason to change it.

Do not unnecessarily redesign those sections.

==================================================
PROGRESS INDICATOR
==================================================

Use ONE overall progress indicator for the integrated assessment.

Do not show separate progress such as:

"APEST complete"

"Starting Spiritual Gifts"

"Strengths 40%"

Instead, show the participant progress through the blended assessment as one experience.

For example:

"Assessment progress: 45%"

The progress calculation must be based only on questions currently active for:

- that church
- that pathway
- that participant

Disabled questions must not count against progress.

==================================================
STABLE ORDER — DO NOT RANDOMIZE EACH ATTEMPT
==================================================

For the first version, use a stable designed order within the mixed rounds.

Do not randomly reshuffle the questions every time someone takes the assessment.

A stable order will make:

- testing easier
- translation easier
- debugging easier
- pilot comparisons easier
- participant feedback more useful
- scoring discrepancies easier to investigate

We can consider controlled randomization later if testing shows a benefit.

==================================================
SAVE AND RESUME
==================================================

Preserve automatic saving.

Participants must be able to:

- leave during the integrated assessment
- return later
- resume at the correct point
- keep all previous answers

Do not lose progress when questions are dynamically generated from church settings.

Store enough information in the assessment snapshot to know exactly which questions were active when the attempt began.

==================================================
N/A HANDLING
==================================================

Keep the existing N/A behavior.

N/A means:

Not sure / no opportunity / not enough experience

N/A must:

- remain unscored
- not become zero
- not lower a construct score
- not count as a negative response

Skipped items should also remain missing rather than zero.

==================================================
HISTORICAL SNAPSHOTS
==================================================

Do not alter historical completed assessments.

Each new integrated assessment should preserve:

- assessment_version
- question_set_version
- scoring_model_version
- enabled_gifts_snapshot
- active_section_snapshot
- active_question_ids
- date started
- date completed

Historical results must remain tied to the configuration and scoring rules that existed when the participant completed them.

==================================================
DO NOT MODIFY YOUTH PATHWAYS YET
==================================================

Do NOT apply the adult integrated assessment design automatically to:

- children
- preteens
- teens
- youth pathways

Leave current youth assessment flows unchanged for now.

Only make compatibility changes if technically necessary.

Once the adult integrated assessment is stable and tested, the youth pathways can be reviewed separately and intentionally adapted by age group.

==================================================
ROUND DESIGN REQUIREMENTS
==================================================

Create approximately 5–6 mixed rounds.

Each round should feel coherent to the participant.

Avoid situations where:

one round is almost entirely APEST

another is almost entirely Personality

another is almost entirely Strengths.

The rounds should feel genuinely blended.

However, do not force a question into a round where it does not naturally fit.

Use thoughtful UX grouping rather than mechanical equal distribution.

==================================================
QUESTION-TO-ROUND LOGIC
==================================================

Assign questions to rounds based on the behavior or experience being described.

For example:

Questions about noticing needs, initiating action, and responding to opportunity could fit under:

HOW YOU NOTICE AND RESPOND

Questions about collaboration, empathy, listening, and relationships could fit under:

HOW YOU WORK WITH OTHERS

Questions about explaining, teaching, influencing, inviting, and communication could fit under:

HOW YOU COMMUNICATE AND GUIDE

Questions about practical service, care, hospitality, encouragement, and support could fit under:

HOW YOU CARE AND SERVE

Questions about discernment, learning, strategy, change, flexibility, and evaluation could fit under:

HOW YOU THINK AND APPROACH CHANGE

Questions about structure, planning, pace, organization, and work style could fit under:

HOW YOU TEND TO WORK

These are examples.

Use the actual final question bank and scoring map to determine the best placement.

==================================================
BODY-METAPHOR TENDENCIES
==================================================

Do not create questions specifically as a fifth body-metaphor assessment.

The six body-metaphor tendencies must remain in:

WHERE THESE THINGS COME TOGETHER

under:

YOUR SERVING PATTERN

They should continue functioning as a synthesis of the four main assessment areas rather than becoming an independent fifth result.

Do not add body-metaphor labels to participant questions.

==================================================
PART FINDER
==================================================

Part Finder should continue receiving and understanding the four assessment categories separately.

Do not flatten the integrated assessment into one data source.

Part Finder should still understand:

APEST is ministry orientation.

Spiritual Gifts are gifts to explore.

Strengths are natural capabilities.

Personality describes how someone tends to operate.

The blended assessment changes how the information is collected, not how Part Finder interprets it.

==================================================
BEFORE BUILDING — PRODUCE A DESIGN REPORT
==================================================

Before modifying the assessment engine, first produce a design/scoping report.

Include:

1. The proposed mixed-round names and order.

2. The number of questions in each round.

3. Every question ID assigned to each round.

4. Which scoring categories and constructs each question contributes to.

5. Which questions disappear if a section is disabled.

6. Which questions remain because they still support another enabled construct.

7. How Spiritual Gift ON/OFF settings affect the blended flow.

8. How progress percentage will be calculated.

9. How save/resume works if church configuration changes after an assessment has started.

10. What gets stored in the assessment snapshot.

11. Confirmation that old profiles remain untouched.

12. Confirmation that youth pathways remain unchanged.

13. Confirmation that the four result sections remain separate.

14. Confirmation that Your Serving Pattern remains synthesis rather than a fifth assessment.

15. Any risks or conflicts found in the current implementation.

Do not begin destructive database changes.

Flag anything uncertain rather than guessing.

==================================================
IMPLEMENT BEHIND PILOT MODE
==================================================

After the design is reviewed, implement the adult integrated assessment behind the existing or new pilot/feature flag.

For example:

integrated_assessment_enabled

integrated_assessment_pilot_mode

Do not immediately remove the old assessment.

Keep the current assessment available during pilot testing.

==================================================
FINAL GOAL
==================================================

The participant experience should feel like:

ONE coherent assessment

followed by the deeper reflective parts of the Ministry Profile.

The system should still understand and present:

HOW YOU TEND TO MINISTER
APEST

HOW GOD HAS EQUIPPED YOU
Spiritual Gifts

WHAT YOU ARE NATURALLY GOOD AT
Strengths

HOW YOU TEND TO OPERATE
Personality

The assessment flow should become simpler.

The profile itself should remain rich.

The goal is not to flatten a person into a single score.

The goal is to reduce unnecessary assessment friction while preserving the depth needed for prayer, discernment, conversation, ministry discovery, and growth.
