## The Story of Every Part

Every Part began with a problem we were experiencing in our own church.

My wife and I found ourselves doing what felt like 90% of the work of the church.

The surprising part was that we were not leading a tiny congregation. We had around 150 people who could participate in the life and ministry of the church. There were people all around us who loved Jesus, cared about the church, and had something meaningful to contribute.

The problem was that I did not know how to connect them with the right opportunities.

I knew the people in our church had gifts. I knew they had passions, experiences, abilities, and stories that God could use. But as a pastor, I often did not have a clear enough picture of how God had shaped each person.

And our church had another layer of complexity.

We are a multicultural church made up of five different language groups.

That meant I needed a way to understand our people across differences in language, culture, age, and background. I did not want language to keep someone’s gifts from being seen. I did not want age to cause someone’s potential to be overlooked. And I did not want ministry opportunities to keep going primarily to the same handful of people simply because they were the people we already knew to ask.

I wanted to know:

**How has God gifted this person?**

**What are they passionate about?**

**What experiences have shaped them?**

**What kind of ministry might bring them joy and help them grow?**

**Where could they meaningfully contribute to the body of Christ?**

That is where the idea for Every Part began.

I created a Ministry Profile for our church that people could complete in their own language. It was designed to help us learn more than simply whether someone was willing to volunteer. We wanted to understand their spiritual gifts, passions, personality, experiences, ministry interests, availability, and the ways God might be preparing them to serve.

The more I worked on it, the more I kept coming back to Paul's picture of the Church in 1 Corinthians 12.

The Church is **one body made up of many parts**.

Every part matters.

And the body is strongest when every part is known, valued, growing, and able to contribute.

That became the heart behind **Every Part**.

Every Part exists to help pastors and ministry leaders better understand the people God has already placed in their churches—so ministry does not have to begin with:

**“Who can we get to fill this spot?”**

Instead, it can begin with:

**“How has God shaped this person, and where might they flourish?”**

What started as a way to better serve our own multicultural church has grown into something I hope can bless other churches too.

Because I do not think our problem is unique.

Churches everywhere are filled with people whom God has gifted and called. Pastors simply need better ways to see those gifts, understand those people, and help them take their next step.

My hope is that Every Part can help churches do exactly that.

Not simply filling volunteer positions.

Not simply administering another assessment.

But helping every person discover that **they have a part in the body—and their part matters.**
