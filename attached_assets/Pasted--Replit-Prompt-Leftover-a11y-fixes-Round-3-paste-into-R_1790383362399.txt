## 2. FAIL — Two more concatenated accessible names
**File:** `artifacts/every-part/src/pages/church-setup.tsx`
**Observed in the live app's accessibility tree** (visual rendering and DOM HTML show proper spaces — the bug is AX-tree only):
- "26spiritual gifts included" — line ~1116: `<span className="church-setup-summary-pill">{enabledGiftCount} spiritual gifts included</span>`
- "40–60min estimated" — line ~1118: `{assessmentEstimate.minimum}–{assessmentEstimate.maximum} min estimated`
**Fix:** As with the previous round, adjacent JSX expressions/text with only JSX whitespace between them are unreliable in the accessibility tree. Then do one final audit of the whole frontend for any remaining value-abutting-word patterns and fix them all.
