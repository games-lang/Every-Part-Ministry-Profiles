# Replit Development Prompt: Build the Develop Profile

Now build the **Develop Profile for ages 13–17**.

This should bridge the gap between the children's Every Part experience and the existing adult Ministry Profile.

Target completion time:

**15–25 minutes**

Make the interface feel appropriate for teenagers rather than young children.

Use the same Every Part branding, but reduce childish illustrations and use a cleaner, more mature design.

Build these sections:

1. Prayer & Calling
2. About Me
3. How I Tend to Operate
4. Spiritual Gifts Exploration
5. Passions
6. Growing With Jesus
7. Calling & Purpose
8. Ministry Interests
9. Availability & Responsibility
10. My Development Plan

For spiritual gifts, explain clearly that assessment results are not definitive.

Use terminology such as:

**“Gifts you may want to explore further.”**

Do not tell a teenager:

“Your spiritual gift is ______.”

The personality section must continue using the original Every Part personality framework rather than Myers-Briggs names, codes, or copyrighted assessment material.

Include exploration of:

* People energy
* Processing style
* Planning/flexibility
* People/logic considerations
* Action/reflection
* Leadership/support tendencies
* Conflict tendencies
* Team preferences

Create descriptive results rather than personality codes.

The Calling & Purpose section should help teenagers consider questions such as:

* What needs or problems affect me deeply?
* Where do I enjoy making a difference?
* What do other Christians see in me?
* Is there something God may be asking me to explore?
* What ministry experiences would I like to try?
* What skills would I like to develop?

For older teenagers, optionally include vocational interests such as:

* Pastoral ministry
* Missions
* Teaching
* Counseling/care
* Nonprofit/community service
* Business
* Healthcare
* Technology
* Creative work
* Other

Make clear that God's calling can be lived out in any vocation and is not limited to professional ministry.

The final Development Plan should contain:

**Strengths I'm Discovering**

**How I Tend to Operate**

**Gifts I May Want to Explore**

**People & Needs I Care About**

**Ministry Experiences I Might Try**

**One Way I Want to Grow With Jesus**

**People Who Can Help Me Grow**

**Questions About Calling I Want to Keep Exploring**

Include this statement:

**You are still growing and discovering how God has made you. These results are a starting point for conversations, prayer, serving, and learning—not a permanent label.**

Maintain the existing youth privacy and permissions structure.

Do not overwrite Discover or Explore profiles if this student completed them previously.

Please complete the Develop Profile and let me test the entire workflow before making significant changes to the adult Ministry Profile.
