Please redesign and improve the public homepage for Every Part.

The goal of the homepage is to make pastors and church leaders quickly understand what Every Part is, why their church needs it, and want to try it.

Do not change the core app functionality, authentication, dashboard, ministry profile logic, database structure, or existing user data unless absolutely necessary for the homepage redesign. Focus primarily on the public-facing homepage and navigation.

EVERY PART BRAND POSITIONING

Every Part is not simply a spiritual gifts test or questionnaire.

Every Part is a ministry mobilization platform that helps churches prayerfully discover how God has shaped their people and connect them with meaningful places to serve and grow.

The main idea is:

“Every person has a part. Help them discover it.”

The site should feel:

* Warm
* Modern
* Clean
* Ministry-focused
* Trustworthy
* Professional enough for pastors and church leaders
* Spiritual without feeling overly decorative or cliché
* Simple and easy to understand

Avoid making the homepage feel like corporate HR software.

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

Use the current Every Part visual identity where appropriate, but improve the overall polish.

Please:

* Create clear visual hierarchy
* Increase whitespace
* Keep paragraphs short
* Use readable typography
* Use cards and icons thoughtfully
* Make CTAs visually obvious
* Avoid excessive animations
* Avoid clutter
* Ensure excellent mobile responsiveness
* Ensure accessibility with good contrast, readable font sizes, semantic headings, keyboard-friendly navigation, and descriptive button labels
* Keep loading performance in mind
* Reuse existing components where sensible instead of unnecessarily rebuilding everything

IMPORTANT

Before changing anything:

1. Review the current homepage and identify reusable components.
2. Preserve all existing working functionality.
3. Do not expose real user or church data.
4. Do not modify authentication behavior unless necessary.
5. Do not remove existing functionality just to simplify the homepage.
6. Keep the redesign consistent with the rest of the application.
7. After completing the redesign, test the homepage on desktop and mobile sizes.
8. Check all buttons and navigation links.
9. Make sure no placeholder text, broken links, fake statistics, or invented testimonials remain.

The final homepage should make a first-time pastor think:

“I understand what this does.”
“This could help our church.”
“I want to see what it looks like.”
“I am willing to try it.”
