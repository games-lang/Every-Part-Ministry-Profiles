Now create the “How You Tend to Minister” framework for Every Part.

This is DIFFERENT from APEST.

APEST answers:
“What kind of contribution or orientation might I bring to the Body?”

How You Tend to Minister answers:
“How do I naturally tend to engage people, responsibilities, and ministry?”

I want this expressed through additional parts of the Body of Christ.

Create an original Every Part framework.

Start with these six categories:

1. HANDS — THE DOER

Core tendency:
“I notice what needs to be done and tend to jump in.”

Common strengths:
practical
helpful
dependable
action-oriented
service-minded

Potential caution:
May begin doing before understanding the deeper need or may carry too much personally.

2. EARS — THE LISTENER

Core tendency:
“I tend to slow down, listen, understand, and make space for people.”

Common strengths:
patient
relational
empathetic
attentive
good one-on-one

Potential caution:
May hesitate to speak or act when action is needed.

3. SHOULDERS — THE SUPPORTER

Core tendency:
“I naturally help carry responsibility and strengthen other people.”

Common strengths:
loyal
steady
supportive
dependable
team-oriented

Potential caution:
May carry responsibilities that should belong to someone else.

4. VOICE — THE COMMUNICATOR

Core tendency:
“I naturally communicate, encourage, explain, inspire, or influence.”

Common strengths:
verbal
encouraging
expressive
comfortable communicating
able to rally others

Potential caution:
May speak before listening enough.

5. ARMS — THE CONNECTOR

Core tendency:
“I tend to bring people together and help people feel welcomed and connected.”

Common strengths:
hospitality
relationship-building
inclusion
networking
team connection

Potential caution:
May prioritize harmony or connection when a difficult conversation is necessary.

6. BACKBONE — THE ORGANIZER

Core tendency:
“I naturally bring structure, order, planning, and stability.”

Common strengths:
administration
planning
follow-through
organization
systems thinking

Potential caution:
May become frustrated with ambiguity, spontaneity, or people who work differently.

IMPORTANT:

Do not assume these six are final.

Review the existing Every Part assessment questions, especially:
- personality / How You Tend to Operate
- spiritual gifts
- service interests
- ministry experiences
- open-response questions

Determine whether these six provide enough differentiation.

If a seventh category is clearly needed, propose it before adding it.

Do NOT create categories simply to make every body part available.

--------------------------------
SCORING
--------------------------------

Create transparent scoring logic.

The result should primarily be determined by “How You Tend to Operate” responses, with other profile information serving only as supporting signals.

Do NOT allow spiritual gifts or APEST to overpower the ministry tendency score. We want these assessments to tell us different things.

Store:
- raw scores for each tendency
- normalized score
- primary tendency
- secondary tendency
- confidence level / score spread

If the top two results are very close, display:

“Primary tendency: Ears — Listener
Secondary tendency: Shoulders — Supporter”

Do not pretend the assessment is more scientifically precise than it is.

Avoid percentages that imply validated psychometric precision unless we have actually validated the instrument.

--------------------------------
RESULT LANGUAGE
--------------------------------

The output should say:

“You tend to minister like the Ears of the Body…”

rather than:

“You ARE the Ears.”

This distinction matters.

Include:

“This picture describes a natural ministry tendency, not a fixed identity. God may call us to serve beyond what comes most naturally.”

--------------------------------
COMBINING WITH APEST
--------------------------------

Build logic capable of combining any APEST orientation with any ministry tendency.

Examples:

Shepherd + Listener
“Shepherding is a strong orientation for you, and listening may be one of the primary ways you care for people.”

Apostle + Organizer
“You may naturally move ministry toward new opportunities while bringing structure that helps new ideas become sustainable.”

Evangelist + Connector
“You may naturally help people encounter Jesus through relationships, hospitality, and connecting people with the Christian community.”

Teacher + Communicator
“You may naturally help others understand truth through clear explanation and communication.”

Prophet + Supporter
“You may notice concerns or spiritual realities others miss while preferring to strengthen leaders and communities rather than always taking center stage.”

Write combination templates carefully but allow AI-assisted personalization from the person's real profile.

Never imply that a particular combination is better or more spiritually mature than another.
