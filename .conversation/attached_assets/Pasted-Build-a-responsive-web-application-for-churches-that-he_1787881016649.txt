Build a responsive web application for churches that helps people discover how God has wired them for ministry and helps church leaders connect people to ministry teams.

For now, use the working name **Every Part**. The name can be changed later.

## Main Purpose

The purpose of the website is to help churches move people from simply attending church to actively serving in a ministry team that fits their gifts, passions, personality, experience, and availability.

The core idea is based on 1 Corinthians 12: every person is an important part of the Body of Christ.

The goal is not to tell people exactly what ministry they must do. The Ministry Profile should help start conversations between church members and church leaders about where they may serve most fruitfully.

Churches should also be able to communicate that even though we want people serving primarily in areas that fit their gifts and passions, there may still be times when they are asked to serve outside their preferred ministry area because of the needs of the church.

## Version 1 Goal

Do NOT try to build a complete church management system.

Version 1 should focus only on:

1. Church accounts
2. Ministry Profile assessments
3. Member results
4. Pastor/admin dashboard
5. Printable/downloadable Ministry Profile reports

Build the system in a way that additional features can be added later.

## User Types

There should initially be two user types:

### Church Administrator / Pastor

A church administrator should be able to:

* Create a church account
* Log in securely
* Enter the church name
* Upload a church logo
* Enter basic church information
* See everyone who has completed a Ministry Profile for their church
* Open an individual person's profile
* View their assessment results and responses
* Generate or print a Ministry Profile report

### Church Member

A church member should NOT need to create a full account initially.

They should be able to:

* Access their church's unique Ministry Profile link
* Enter their name and basic information
* Complete the assessment
* Submit the profile
* See a clean results page after submission

## Multi-Church Structure

This is extremely important.

The application must be built as a multi-tenant system.

Every church must have its own private data.

A pastor from Church A must NEVER be able to see members, profiles, or information belonging to Church B.

Every church, member profile, assessment response, and report must be connected to a unique church ID.

Build database security and authorization around this from the beginning.

## Main Pages

Create these pages first:

### 1. Public Home Page

Create a professional landing page explaining the product.

Include:

Headline:
**Help Every Person Find Their Place in the Body of Christ**

Subheadline:
**Every Part helps churches discover the gifts, passions, strengths, and ministry potential of their people—and connect them to meaningful places of service.**

Include sections explaining:

* Discover how God has wired your people
* Understand their gifts and passions
* Give pastors meaningful ministry insights
* Help people find ministry teams where they can thrive
* Move people from attendance to participation

Include buttons:

**For Churches**
**Take a Ministry Profile**
**Church Login**

Do not include pricing yet.

### 2. Church Signup Page

Allow a church administrator to create an account using:

* First name
* Last name
* Email
* Password
* Church name

After signup, create a unique church record.

### 3. Church Setup Page

Allow the administrator to enter:

* Church name
* Church logo
* Church address
* Website
* Primary contact name
* Primary contact email

The church should automatically receive a unique Ministry Profile URL or code.

Example concept:

everypart.com/profile/church-name

Do not worry about the final domain yet.

### 4. Ministry Profile Assessment Page

Use a multi-step form with a progress bar rather than one extremely long page.

* Email
* Phone
* Age range
* Preferred contact method
* Family situation
* Transportation availability

#### Church Connection

* How long have you attended this church?
* How connected do you currently feel to the church? 1–5
* How long have you been following Jesus?
* Have you served in ministry before?
* If yes, where?

* Immigrants and refugees
* Multicultural ministry

* People experiencing addiction
* People who are hurting or grieving
* Community outreach

#### Ministry Interests

Allow multiple selections such as:

## Results Page

After a member submits the Ministry Profile, show a clean results page.

For Version 1 show:

* Member name
* Top selected passions
* Top ministry interests
* Availability
* Skills and experience
* Basic church connection information

Also show this message:

**Your Ministry Profile is a starting point, not a box.**

God has uniquely shaped you through your gifts, passions, experiences, personality, relationships, and story. Your profile can help you and your church leaders prayerfully discover where you may serve most fruitfully.

Include:

**Your Next Step**

Encourage the person to talk with a pastor or ministry leader about their results and possible places to serve.

## Pastor Dashboard

Create a secure dashboard.

The dashboard should show:

* Church name and logo
* Number of completed Ministry Profiles
* Recently completed profiles
* Search members by name
* Filter members by ministry interest
* Filter members by passion
* Filter members by availability

Use a clean table or card layout.

Each member should have a button:

**View Profile**

## Individual Member Profile

When a pastor opens a member profile, display:

* Basic information
* Church connection
* Passions
* Ministry interests
* Availability
* Experience
* Skills
* Assessment sections
* Date completed

Include a section called:

**Possible Ministry Conversations**

For now, simply identify several ministry areas based on the person's selected interests and passions.

Do not use AI or advanced matching yet.

## Printable Ministry Profile

Create a clean print-friendly report.

It should include:

* Church logo
* Member name
* Ministry Profile title
* Passions
* Ministry interests
* Availability
* Skills
* Experience
* Church connection
* Future assessment results

Include this statement near the beginning:

**Our goal is to help every person become part of a ministry team that fits their God-given gifts, passions, experiences, and strengths. However, following Jesus also means being willing to serve others when needs arise, even when the task may fall outside our primary area of gifting.**

Make the report suitable for printing on standard 8.5 x 11 paper.

## Design Style

Use a modern, warm, church-friendly design.

Avoid looking overly corporate or like generic business software.

Use:

* Plenty of white space
* Large readable headings
* Soft rounded cards
* Clear navigation
* Mobile-responsive layouts
* Professional typography

The design should communicate:

* belonging
* purpose
* ministry
* discipleship
* community
* warmth

Do not use denomination-specific branding so churches from different denominations could use it.

## Technology

Choose a modern, reliable web stack that works well on Replit.

Use:

* Secure authentication
* A relational database
* Proper authorization
* Responsive frontend design

If Replit's built-in database and authentication tools are appropriate, use them. If an external service is needed, explain why before adding unnecessary dependencies.

Keep the architecture simple enough that a non-programmer can maintain and continue developing it with Replit Agent.

## Important Development Instructions

Do not build everything at once.

First:

1. Create the project structure
2. Build authentication
3. Build the church database structure
4. Build the public landing page
5. Build the basic church dashboard

Make sure those pieces work before continuing into the full Ministry Profile assessment.

Keep the code organized and well documented.

At each major development step, explain in plain English what was created and what I should test before moving to the next step.
