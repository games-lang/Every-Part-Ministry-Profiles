Revised Integrated Assessment — Content & Coverage Report

Proposal status: content-design and validation material only. No application, database, configuration, live assessment, or participant experience was changed. This is an adult-only pilot proposal, not an approved instrument, diagnosis, placement recommendation, or psychometric claim. The deployed production system was not independently checked; the exact 5 / 26 / 18 / 7 taxonomy was read from artifacts/every-part/src/pages/assessment.tsx.

1. Recommendation and scope

Recommendation: review before implementation. The revised bank closes the defined content-count gaps for all proposed assessment-scored ordinary Gifts and Strengths and gives every APEST orientation six strong indicators. It is outside the requested 65–75 range: 83 core questions, or 8 over 75. The overage comes from 10 APEST additions (44–53) needed to raise four orientations from three to six, plus nine focused additions for prayer/intercession, hospitality, practical service, music, problem-solving, faith, and giving (54–57; 58A–62A), while retaining 21 independent personality items. A 75-core tradeoff would require removing eight focused additions and would reopen multiple three-indicator coverage gaps; it is a review choice, not an optimization proof. It still needs cognitive interviews, translation review, pilot timing/N/A analysis, and validation before any score is published.

Four results remain separate: APEST / How You Tend to Minister; Gifts / How God Has Equipped You; Strengths / What You Are Naturally Good At; and Personality / How You Tend to Operate. Serving Pattern remains a synthesis, not a fifth scored area. A high score in one category never supplies evidence for another; cross-category overlap is correlated evidence, not independent confirmation.

APEST (5): Apostle, Prophet, Evangelist, Shepherd, Teacher. Personality spectra (7): Social Energy, Decision Lens, Planning Style, Focus Style, Action Style, Pace Preference, Work Style.

Reflection response values are 1–5 (Not at all / A little / Sometimes / Often / Very much like me) plus unscored N/A — Not sure / I have not had the opportunity. Bipolar personality values are 1–5 from the named left pole through balanced to the right pole, with the same N/A. ESD uses the stated special N/A: Not sure / I have not experienced this / this is not part of my tradition.

5. Primary/secondary evidence rules

Primary maps have numeric weight 1.0; secondary maps have 0.5. A secondary is a narrower, behavior-supported relationship, never a filler and never a publishing shortcut. Q46 planning supports strategic thinking only secondarily; it does not prove follow-through. Neutral Action Style does not infer Apostle or Leadership. Humility, guidance, or openness in ESD does not infer supernatural experience. Generic kindness is not used as evidence of Evangelism. Each construct is calculated independently as Σ(response × answered-map weight) / Σ(answered-map weights); N/A and skip contribute neither numerator nor denominator.

15. Cultural, access, and multilingual review

  • Leadership/authority: Q44–46 and Q12–14 must not reward speaking publicly, individualism, or formal authority; community-led and quiet forms of action must be recognized.
  • Education/communication: Q28–33 and Q53 need plain translated equivalents for “faith idea,” “clarify,” and “insight”; formal schooling must not be a proxy for teaching ability.
  • Culture and access: Q03/Q18/Q19 must not assume travel, majority-culture expertise, or a particular church welcome process. Q35/Q58A/Q59A require opportunity-sensitive N/A. Q55 does not require a home, money, or hosted event.
  • Economics and family status: Q37–39 are conditional only and never a value judgment. Q40–42/Q62A must never score income, disposable resources, or deprivation as spirituality.
  • Faith tradition: ESD language is self-described (“I believe,” “may have”) and includes the exact unscored tradition-sensitive N/A. No item asserts supernatural certainty.

16. Publishing threshold and evidence guardrails

Proposed requested threshold: publish a construct only with at least three distinct answered meaningful mapped IDs; primary and secondary counts and 1.0/0.5 weighted opportunities remain visible separately. Three answers are a reporting threshold, not psychometric validity and not three independent observations. For every construct the UI must show the actual numerator / denominator from answered mapped items, label missing/N/A as excluded, and show “Not enough information yet” when the threshold is not met. A stricter three-primary gate is an optional, unapproved future review policy only; it is not used to change these maps or to call secondary-heavy constructs threshold failures.
