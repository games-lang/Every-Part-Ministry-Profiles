## Read the whole story

From [People](/profiles), open a completed person's profile to see the leader-facing view. The adult profile unfolds as chapters of one ministry story: the named areas **APEST**, **Gifts**, **Strengths**, and **Personality** provide evidence; the overall picture draws them together for conversation. The exact content varies with the person's answers and the church's assessment settings.

Use what the person actually shared, including experience and availability, rather than treating a single result as their identity. A missing answer may appear as **Not shared**. Leader-only details should stay in the leader view, not in a public invitation.

For the participant's own perspective, see [what stood out in your responses](/help/response-patterns).
