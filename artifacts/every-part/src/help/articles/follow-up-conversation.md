## Begin with the person

After reading a completed profile in [People](/profiles), invite the participant to reflect with you. Consider asking:

- “Which part of this sounds like you? Which part misses something important?”
- “What kinds of serving have brought you joy or felt difficult?”
- “What would you like to try, learn, or pray about next?”

Look at their current availability and preferences before discussing a team. Use APEST, Gifts, Strengths, and Personality as prompts for discernment, not labels or a verdict about calling. It is fine to leave the conversation open and revisit it later.
