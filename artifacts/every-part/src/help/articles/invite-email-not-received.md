## First check how the link was sent

Every Part does **not** automatically send an email when you add someone in [People](/profiles). **Email link** opens a pre-addressed message in *your* email app; you must send that message yourself. You can also choose **Copy link** and share it by your usual channel.

If the message was sent but the person cannot find it, check the address, your Sent folder, and their spam folder. The private invitation expires after 30 days. If the People status says **Invite expired**, choose **Renew** and share the new link. A church-wide [assessment link](/help/assessment-link) is another option if you do not need a person-specific invitation.
