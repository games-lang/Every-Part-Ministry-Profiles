## Start in People

Open [People](/profiles) and choose **Add person** under **Invite someone to reflect**. Enter their details and select **Add person & create link**. The private link can be copied, or **Email link** can open a pre-addressed message in your own email app. Every Part does not send the email for you.

You can also share the general [assessment link](/help/assessment-link) from Church Setup, or use **Import CSV** to add several people at once. Check each person's status in People: **Awaiting profile**, **Invite expired**, or **Profile complete**.

When the first person finishes, open their completed profile from People. [Reading the first results](/help/read-first-results) explains what to look for.
