## One link for one person

In [People](/profiles), **Add person** creates a person record and a private assessment invitation. Use **Copy link** to share it yourself, or **Email link** to open your email app with a message ready to send. The app does not deliver invitation emails automatically.

An invitation expires after 30 days. If it shows **Invite expired**, use **Renew** in People and send the new link. The older link will not work. The person can complete their own assessment from the link; a pending invitation does not create a completed profile.

For a link not tied to a person record, use [Your Assessment Link](/help/assessment-link) instead.
