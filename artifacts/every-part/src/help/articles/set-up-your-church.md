## Finish the basics

After creating your church account, enter your church name, your name, and a contact email. Choose a starting list of spiritual gifts; you can adjust it later. The [Church Setup](/church-setup) page is where you manage your church details and the assessment people will see.

## Check before sharing

- In **Church Setup**, review your church details and the **Assessment Setup** tab. You can choose which sections appear and which spiritual gifts to include.
- The adult integrated assessment is the default. In Church Setup you can choose the classic adult assessment instead. A draft already in progress keeps the format and questions it started with.
- Find **Your Assessment Link** on the Church Setup tab. Copy it and open it yourself to check the entry page before sharing.

Your first step after setup is to [invite someone to reflect](/help/invite-first-people).
