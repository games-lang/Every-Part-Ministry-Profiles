## Share the church link

Open [Church Setup](/church-setup) and find **Your Assessment Link** on the **Church Setup** tab. Select **Copy**, then paste the link into a message you send to your congregation. The nearby open-link button lets you check the entry page.

The link leads to your church's participant entry, where age determines the appropriate path. Adults see your church's current assessment format; younger participants use their age-specific paths. The adult integrated format is the default unless your church chose classic in **Assessment Setup**.

If you want to track a specific person as awaiting a profile in People, [create a private invitation](/help/private-invitations) instead.
