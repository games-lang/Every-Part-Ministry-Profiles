## What AI does—and does not do

The assessment reflects answers the participant provided; the named result areas and response patterns come from those answers. They are prompts for discussion, not an AI decision about a person's calling or team placement.

The optional **AI-assisted Profile Helper** in the leader profile can suggest ways to discuss selected profile signals. It uses selected signals, **not** names, contact details, private notes, life experiences, or spiritual-health responses. It can make mistakes. Leaders should check its suggestions against what the person actually said and use their own judgment.

AI credits are tracked separately from profile capacity on [Plan & usage](/billing). Do not enter private or sensitive information into the public AI Guide.
