## Patterns, not a verdict

In the integrated adult experience, **What stood out in your responses** offers a gentle summary of themes drawn from the participant's answers. It is not a score for faithfulness, a ministry assignment, or a fixed personality label. Skipped and “N/A” answers are not scored.

In the leader-facing profile, compare the patterns with the person's own words, experience, and availability. Church choices determine which reflection areas a new participant sees; an already completed profile retains the settings and wording from when it was submitted.

Ask the person which themes fit and which do not. The most useful next step is a [follow-up conversation](/help/follow-up-conversation), not a placement based on one screen.
