## Plans are coming later

The [Plan & usage](/billing) page shows monthly and annual planned prices and your current usage. Plan cards say **Coming later**. Every Part is not currently processing live payments, and no payment account is required for ordinary early-access churches.

There is currently **no self-service invoice history, upgrade, cancellation, or payment portal** in this dashboard. Do not expect a charge or an invoice from the planned prices shown here. When paid subscriptions are made available, the page will need to explain how to choose a plan and manage billing; until then, a disabled plan card cannot change your church's plan.

For profile capacity and what happens at a limit, see [plans and limits](/help/plans-and-limits).
