## What Plan & usage shows

[Plan & usage](/billing) lists planned monthly tiers: **Starter $0** (5 profiles), **Growing $10** (50), **Complete $20** (100), **Network $30** (250), and **Unlimited $50** (no profile cap). The page also shows your current AI credit usage. These prices and capacities are visible for planning; live paid checkout is not open.

Ordinary churches can create and view unlimited Ministry Profiles during early access. Where a profile visibility limit applies, people can **still submit** an assessment after the limit; leaders cannot view over-limit results until the church moves to enough capacity. No completed profiles are deleted by hitting a limit.

AI credits have a separate monthly limit; check the usage card for your current balance.
