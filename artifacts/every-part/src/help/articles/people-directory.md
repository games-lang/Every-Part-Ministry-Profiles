## Keep track of invitations and profiles

[People](/profiles) is the admin area for church people, invitations, and completed profiles; it is not called “Profiles” in the dashboard navigation. You can **Add person** and create a link, or **Import CSV** to add several people. The directory shows whether someone is **Awaiting profile**, has an **Invite expired**, or is **Profile complete**.

Open a completed person to read their results and discuss next steps. Pending people do not have finished assessment results. Use [Teams](/teams) separately for ministry-team organization.

If a record should be removed, read [teams and removal](/help/teams-and-removal) first: removal is permanent.
