## What is collected

An assessment stores the contact details and answers a participant chooses to share, plus the resulting profile. Optional answers may include experiences, spiritual reflections, availability, and a photo if enabled by the church. Church leaders can access their church's completed profiles; results are not a public directory. The [Privacy page](/privacy) explains the broader policy.

## Deleting a record

A participant should ask their church to remove their data. A church leader can use **Remove person** in People or the completed profile and confirm **Permanently remove**. This deletes the linked person, invitation, profile responses/results, assignment, shifts, notes, and photo; a private journey is removed only if nothing else shares it. This action cannot be undone.

For an unfinished adult integrated draft, the participant can instead choose **Discard draft and start fresh** in the same browser.
