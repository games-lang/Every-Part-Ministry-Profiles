## Continue on the same device

In the integrated adult assessment, a draft is saved in the browser where it was started and can be resumed from the landing page with **Resume saved profile**. The saved attempt keeps its original questions and church settings. It expires after 30 days. Starting in a different browser or clearing that browser's storage may leave the draft unavailable there.

If you need to start over, use **Discard draft and start fresh** and confirm. Discarding the unfinished draft removes its saved answers; it does not delete a completed profile. If a save or discard reports an error, retry rather than assuming the draft was removed.

Other age pathways can have different draft rules. Check the message shown on that participant's screen.
