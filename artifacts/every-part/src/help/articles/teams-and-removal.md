## Ministry Teams

Open [Teams](/teams) to create and manage ministry teams. You can edit a team or archive and restore it; archiving a team keeps its member assignments rather than deleting their profiles. Completed profiles can be connected to a current team through the leader tools.

## Remove a person permanently

From People or a completed profile, choose **Remove person** and read the confirmation before selecting **Permanently remove**. This cannot be undone. It removes the person and invitation, their linked completed profile and results, and associated team assignment, scheduled shifts, private notes, and photo. A private journey is removed only if no other profile shares it.

If you only need to stop using a team, archiving the team is different from removing a person.
