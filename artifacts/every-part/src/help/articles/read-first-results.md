## Open a completed profile

Go to [People](/profiles) and open a person marked **Profile complete**. The leader-facing profile tells one ministry story rather than delivering a placement decision. Read the named APEST, Gifts, Strengths, and Personality areas alongside the person's own answers and context. Some sections may say **Not shared** if the person did not answer.

The participant may also open their private Ministry Profile journey link after submitting. Your leader view and their private journey are not interchangeable.

Use the [follow-up conversation guide](/help/follow-up-conversation) to ask what resonates, what needs more context, and where the person wants to grow. Do not treat a pattern as a calling or a requirement to join a team.
