## From link to profile

Participants opening the church link first choose the path appropriate for their age. Adults see a church-branded introduction with an estimated time, then **Begin**. The integrated adult assessment moves through personal details, reflections, and the other sections your church has enabled. It ends with a review and **Submit profile**. A church using the classic adult format shows its classic questions instead.

Adult integrated progress can be saved in the same browser; the participant can resume or explicitly discard that draft. A finished participant sees a completion message and a private link to their Ministry Profile. Youth paths have their own age and guardian safeguards.

Questions and estimated time depend on the church's settings; avoid promising everyone the same number of questions.
