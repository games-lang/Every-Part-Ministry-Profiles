# My Leadership and PartFinder

My Leadership lets you save the ministry priorities, leadership context, and communication preferences you want PartFinder to use. It belongs to your church account and you can review or edit it on the My Leadership page.

## Separate from your Ministry Profile

Your Ministry Profile reflects your assessment responses. My Leadership is separate: it is context you choose to share for leadership support, not an assessment or a new score. It does not change anyone's assessment results.

## What it shapes

- **PartFinder guidance:** Your saved context helps personalize guidance. Your coaching style and preferred answer length shape its tone and detail.
- **Find-people prioritization:** Delegation needs, areas to strengthen, and priorities can give a small local boost to people whose existing profile signals connect with those areas. People still need relevant evidence for the ministry need; this is not a placement decision. PartFinder tells you when your leadership profile helped prioritize the returned results.
- **Team suggestions:** Areas to strengthen and priorities help prioritize related profile signals, including when AI is unavailable. A focus you enter for that request takes precedence. The summary tells you when your My Leadership priorities shaped suggestions, or when no shared signals matched.

The unassigned list and church-wide insights are not personalized by My Leadership.

## Pause personalization

Turn off personalization on the My Leadership page and save. Your information stays saved, but it is not used for guidance, find-people prioritization, or team suggestions. Guidance uses the balanced style and standard answer length while paused. PartFinder shows a quiet personalization status link when you have a saved profile.

## Share thoughtfully

What you write here is shared with the AI assistant to personalize guidance — please avoid sensitive personal details about other people. For find-people matching and team suggestions, leadership text is compared locally and is not sent to the AI.

PartFinder suggestions are conversation starters. Review profiles, pray, and talk with people before inviting them to serve.
